# Local Godot acceptance checklist

**All rows are NOT_RUN in this handoff.** Only the integration lead schedules the real game. Save baseline and candidate source identities, exact inputs, control/profile state, camera setting, capture timestamps and normal/reduced setting. Our offline boards are references, not evidence for these rows.

| Check | Capture / observation required |
|---|---|
| Whole-cell truth | Show selected corner coverage beyond the original selection circle; a nonconvex union, isolated cell and a hole. Compare actual affected cells with visible full-cell material; no smoothed hull, new cells or spill. |
| Phase boundary | Capture frames immediately before/after source activation, decay and expiry. Use unmodified terminal Water/Fire/Steam references, then a trail and modified Steam. Water remains non-damaging; hot cues end at actual danger=false; no stale residue after expiry. |
| Readability matrix | Actual 1280×720 captures at the game's 50/75/100 settings, light/dark floors, normal/reduced, all three body templates and representative eight-direction aim/movement overlaps. Preserve hands, actor outline, aim and small projectiles. Do not mark the whole matrix accepted from one frame. |
| Steam identity | Show forming, ordinary, heated, cooled and harmless decay. Actors remain visible. Two actors with different authoritative exposures in one field must not share a fabricated cloud-age meter. Missing binding disables the optional actor ticks. |
| Overlap / ownership | Same-record neighbors join; different owners, footprints, intensities, phases and expiry times keep meaningful seams. Active danger must remain visible alongside harmless decay and Water. Cooled dangerous Steam keeps its scald cue. |
| Spell / reaction separation | Water versus Flood; Fire versus Conflagration; independent Steam identity. Exercise Bolt/Heavy/Rapid/Spray and strength loss. Rapid has no persistent trail. Fixed hit geometry/budgets remain unchanged. |
| State and load | Re-entry, protection/air height, refreshed observations, late join and expiry must follow source authority. Run the lead's focused regression plus required broader gate; report real demand, admitted/refused work and event accounting before interpreting performance. |
| Render / human gate | Profile a comparable rendered workload with source identity and frame-time distribution. Collect human readability/style feedback at native scale. Record failures/open issues. No checkpoint promotion from offline PNGs, copied historic Full results or headless timing alone. |
