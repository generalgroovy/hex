Unmodified historical source captures

Formation is not damage. Decay is not danger.

Each lifecycle state is shown on two floor values, at native 100% fixture scale. The three samples within each phase vary only the motif.

Water terminal reference: 24 forming + 636 active reagent + 60 decay = 720 ticks. No damage is added.

Fire terminal reference: 24 ignition + 396 dangerous + 60 harmless cooling = 480 ticks.

Unmodified Steam reference: 24 forming + 360 dangerous + 60 harmless decay = 444 ticks. Heat modifiers can change the real interval.

120 Hz reference only. Read actual live phase/timestamps. Trail lifetimes and modified products are not governed by new timers in this proposal.

Exact coverage, including overlaps

Same-record neighbors join. Different ownership remains visible. Actor-specific scald rises without turning the field opaque.

No circle fallback. Use all six received cell vertices. Outlines are drawn inward; cropped motifs do not change coverage.

Heat ≠ exposure. Heat belongs to the Steam region. The three actor ticks are a read-only exposure proposal, never a cloud-age timer.

No false safety. Cooled Steam can still scald. Without actor exposure data, omit the exposure meter rather than estimating it.
