## 4. Two important static deductions

### Vortex's eye is filled by the selection rule

The hex has opposing top/bottom vertices 73,900 world units apart. A radius-30,000 hole has a diameter of only 60,000, so no complete hex can fit in it. The annulus selector returns true whenever the outer disk overlaps a hex with positive area and any vertex lies outside the inner circle. Every hex necessarily has such a vertex. Consequently the baseline annulus selects the same cells as its 90 px outer disk, including the center-owning cell. A possible special case in direction normalization at one exact point is not a usable safe region. [G:L20-L26; F:L720-L736]

E01 changes only the effective inner radius to 74,000 units. The largest distance between any two points in the authored hex is below 74,000, so the whole hex containing the exact reaction center fits inside that hole even when the center is near a face or vertex. No selected cell is partially protected. This guarantees a geometric exclusion, not an unobstructed physical escape route: cover, walls and small-space behavior still require Godot tests. [G:L20-L26; evidence/STATIC_CHECKS.json]

### Weak cooling can increase computed scald

Consider a pristine Bolt/Bolt Steam product: total quantity 1200, intensity 500. An independently calculated unobstructed radius-64 px selection at a grid center contains seven cells. Canonical equal allocation gives its first cell 172 units. At exposure 120, the current formula yields **1075 health units (1.075 HP)** for that product.

A fresh Rapid Water source contributes strength 1 and 600 matter. Cooling lowers intensity to 450, but increases quantity to 1800 and that cell's allocation to 258. At the **same** exposure, the formula yields **1451 health units (1.451 HP)**—more scald after nominal cooling. These numbers are integer arithmetic from supplied formulas, not observed hits. They do not establish average combat damage; selection occlusion, exposure, pulse alignment and a stronger overlapping Fire winner can change the actual outcome. [W:L401-L426; W:L669-L675; W:L904-L913; evidence/STATIC_CHECKS.json]

E05 proposes a real paid cooling payoff: consume the Water source through the existing ledger, reduce intensity by the current rule, but account that source as consumed cooling work rather than extra scald-carrying product matter. The product's pre-cooling quantity and coverage remain unchanged. At equal exposure, lower intensity then cannot raise that product's damage. This is an explicit proposed policy change, not a claim that current code already does it. No refund, free Water deposit or new reaction type is introduced.

## 7. Family redundancy, size and harassment assessment

Delivery is not reaction identity. Bolt/Rapid terminal disks differ in setup reach; Heavy has a separate blast and stronger subtraction; Spray offers instantaneous authored-sector interception. Their produced reaction footprint usually does not shrink with family strength. Thus a weak delivery can still acquire full-size Mud, Rampart, Vortex or fixed-damage Magma. This is a credible utility-per-cost problem, but the missing actual Flux/rate/recovery table prevents a defensible “Rapid is dominant” verdict. E04 asks that question narrowly instead of inventing DPS numbers. [W:L204-L227; W:L479-L555; E:L351-L368]

Current live display sizes are 58/63/68 px, hurt radii 17/18/19 px and common wall clearance 18 px; the body JSON's 58/68/76 values are authored source envelopes, not an authorization to enlarge live bodies. Its stat ranges are not exact chosen champion stats. Environmental membership follows the foot; direct shots and Heavy blast depend on continuous target geometry. Compare identical trajectories first, then real-size mobility/resource profiles, to distinguish geometry unfairness from intentional survivability tradeoffs. No experiment here changes size, stamina, health or base movement. [CONTRACT:L19-L19; SPEC:L666-L676; BODY:L6-L52; C:L613-L625]

Spawn harassment is not disproved by a protection check. Diagnose pre-laid ground effects at protection expiry, Rampart creation enclosing a protected foot, multi-field flow beside a wall, and pressure while a station screen is open. Do not invent menu invulnerability or a forbidden chemistry region. The acceptance question is whether an available, taught escape action actually works without unseen size-dependent blockage.
