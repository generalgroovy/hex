# Element Garden: real ground demonstrations

Status: **implemented and locally qualified in p60-6d80c3bce71e, October 9, 2026.**
The [final integration record](../element-expansion-evidence-20261009/integration.md)
links the passed Full/native/package gates. Native map images inspect arrivals,
menus, map pages, garden phases and paid-ground overlaps; they do not establish human wayfinding or full district art acceptance.

The garden has 63 authored physical plots, one for each nonempty six-element
set. The six singleton plots alternate between their single raw deposit and
the actual two-source matching reaction. The other 57 plots show their actual
mixed reaction. A complete walk therefore exposes six raw materials and all
63 current recipe identities, rather than treating the six self reactions as
missing combinations. Entries are ordered by ingredient count and then stable
mask. The map owner provides the validated centers and a travel destination.

Each visible plot owns an isolated `HexChemistryWorld`. Exhibition supply uses
the ordinary reservation, commitment, source binding, terminal deposition,
reaction and expiry paths. Every new source contributes exactly 600 matter;
a mixed payload remains one source, while a self reaction consumes two.
Source strength and the damage pool come from the compiled Bolt definition.
The exhibition does not call `_create_product` directly, retime active phases,
restore spent stock, or expose live actors to its damage/control policies.
It accepts no live world, player, progression, storage or reward reference.
This is a harmless exhibit supplied by the demonstration, not a claim that the
player paid Flux or successfully executed the spell. Spell Range remains the
place to test full combat interactions and payment.

Each plot's eligibility is a four-ring disk of 61 real hex cells. Long fields
are genuinely clipped by that finite authority, so this is explicitly a
**bounded exhibit**, not a full-size footprint demonstration. The largest
vertex lies about 108.22 pixels from the center. Authored plots use a 288-pixel
lattice and 160-pixel clearance from real walls; their 108-pixel pad outline
has a subpixel outer fringe, and neighboring effects remain separate.
No garden pad is a new gameplay obstacle or hazard.

A visible plot follows the actual formation, active and decay clocks. Raw
material phase words come from `render_regions`, including Fire's ignition
and cooling labels. After actual expiry, a 24-tick reset interval precedes a
fresh source identity. Nearby inspection names the ingredients, current field,
phase, time until the next phase, finite lifetime, effect, counter, and harmless
scope. It does not claim a single raw ingredient already has its self-pair
control effect. The six raw descriptions make that distinction explicit.

Offscreen authorities are released. All visible plots are admitted, with a
hard ceiling of the entire authored 63 plots, or 3,843 cells. The reviewed
1280x720 view at 75% camera zoom intersects at most 40 plot envelopes. Larger
desktop windows can show all 63; there is no nearest-only truncation that
silently leaves visible plots empty. Mode transitions retire exhibition state
while preserving the validated directory for a fresh visit.

The focused suite `element-garden-exhibition` exercises real source ledgers,
all six raw deposits, all 63 formed recipe IDs, exact 600/1200 stock,
compiled-strength funding, complete expiry before reset, unique source IDs,
production Hex renderer polygons, all 69 inspection states, actual-font card
bounds, detached metadata, malformed layout refusal, and full-garden visibility.
These are deterministic structural and simulation checks. They do not prove
human comprehension, visual comfort, frame rate or competitive balance.
