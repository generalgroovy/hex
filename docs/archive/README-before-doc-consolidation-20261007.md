# H.E.X. — Help, Everything eXploded!

A movement-led elemental sandbox for Windows, built in Godot. Move and aim
independently, chain paid evasions, configure twelve spell positions, and combine
finite elemental effects across the Wellspring.

**Balance direction:** every element, spell, ancestry and champion must be
independently useful, distinct and counterable. This is a requirement, not yet
a claim that every choice is balanced. [Current audit, gaps and acceptance](docs/HEX-BALANCE-CONTRACT.md)
separate the 32 live spells / 30 characters / 21 ancestries / 36 reactions from
competitive balance and authored uniqueness. A separate [balance-fix candidate](docs/balance-evidence-20261007/README.md)
`p57-c5f23a22d58f` passes 220 suites / 3,886,647 assertions and portable startup /
safe close. [Try the candidate](exports/hex-balance-20261007/windows/PLAY-HEX.cmd)
at 1280x720; 960x540 small-text readability remains degraded. It fixes Penumbra
cadence and clarifies Loom information, not all-choice balance. The selected
team checkpoint and installer below are preserved; no new installer/promotion.
