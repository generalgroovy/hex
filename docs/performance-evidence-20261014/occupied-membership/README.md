# Occupied membership: withdrawn, never applied

The same-draw membership shortcut passed its compatibility and paid-render checks but failed its performance gate. **Keep canonical `p61-64590c45c7a5` unchanged.** All eight primary case/process combinations missed the predeclared 3% saving requirement; dense Fire reduced also reversed direction by invocation order in both processes. No repeated other-case p95 regression exceeded 5%, but that does not override the failed primary gate.

The gate required both at least 50 µs and 3% mean saving in every primary case in each process, a positive paired median, consistent invocation-order direction, and no repeated material negative-control tail regression. The [exact predeclared gate](inputs/acceptance-before-timing.json), [full numerical analysis](analysis/paired-analysis-c.json), [Technical review](analysis/independent-review-c.json) and [independent gameplay/archive review](review.json) retain the decision and limits.

The final [functional qualification](runs/verify-functional-d/receipt.json) passed 1,262 checks across 13 prepared compatibility/path cases, 28 images and 24/24 released viewports. The [forward](runs/probe-forward-c/receipt.json) and [reverse](runs/probe-reverse-c/receipt.json) processes each passed 8,612 checks across 22 paid snapshots, 12 timed states, 32 measured pairs plus four warmups per timed state, 44 PNGs and 2/2 released viewports. All final parser/native stderr files are empty. The gameplay review independently checked 6,289 conditions, including 768 measured pairs, 96 warmup pairs, 88 timing-image hashes, 338 source pins and matching paid/case boundaries across processes. Source and pixel proofs establish this experiment's correctness; they do not make the optimization worthwhile.

Actual four preparation routes produced 2/12/12/2 admissions, **zero refusals**, 1,200/7,200/7,200/1,200 paid matter and complete natural expiry, with no trajectory refills or timer/status injection. The active Mantle snapshot was selected after 4,200 paid matter; that is not the full route's 7,200 total. These readiness-spaced preparation routes differ from Profile B, which recorded six Mantle refusals. No cross-workload speedup or refusal comparison is claimed.

None reached paid timing; none is a gameplay crash. Final sources are preserved as text, with exact input pins, transform proofs, runner evidence and source origins in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Timing covers native offscreen elapsed ground submission only. It does not measure thread CPU directly, GPU/present latency, whole-game FPS, physical input or human feel. Shared cache lifetime, phase-correlated samples, invocation order and host-memory pressure remain limitations. No subsequent optimization or canonical promotion is authorized by this archive.
