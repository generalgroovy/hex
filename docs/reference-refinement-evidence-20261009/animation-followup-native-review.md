## Scope and retained limitations

The 13 sequence frames sample one second at 12 Hz. Their global rendering time stays at 9000; the arranged authority fixtures progress actual vertical velocity and remaining flight ticks. They are not a physical movement replay: actor height/resource trajectories, input timing, collisions and networking are not being qualified by this board. An ordinary hop need not last the whole descriptor envelope or perform a full 360-degree turn. The renderer removes the tuck immediately on landing. Cast effects remain, but no independently authored aerial casting-hand overlay is claimed. The original atlas bytes are untouched; compact anatomy is assembled from native parts rather than newly painted per-character animation.

The actual terrain texture is admitted against the existing Wellspring manifest RGBA hash, and exact 128px swatches are used. This is not whole-map composition or an in-game camera capture. At75%, bright protection shapes are identifiable but tiny pip counts and costume details remain a limited visual sample.
