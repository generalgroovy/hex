# Paid slide chains

Status: SOURCE_CANDIDATE; root owns import, focused/full tests and capture. This worker has not run Godot. No human feel or balance acceptance is claimed. 2026-10-09.

The user explicitly requested more dynamic movement after the earlier conservative momentum repair. Two connected changes now make existing Jump and Slide gestures form a practical travel chain. There are no new buttons, player fields, protection windows or resource refills.

| Decision | Established mechanism and reported result | Transfer and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Add 90 units/s once on a successfully paid Slide, capped at the existing 900 units/s maximum. | [Respawn's controls interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designer-interview-getting-i-titanfall-i-s-controls-just-right) describes a small entry boost and restricted slide turning/stopping; Haggerty reports an enthusiastic internal response to the prototype. | Sprint-to-Slide gains useful speed without erasing ordinary movement. Exact 90 is a new H.E.X. candidate, not a value or measured optimum from Titanfall. The existing 225 entry gate, 22 base Stamina, chain premium and 780 ms cooldown stay authoritative. Braking never renews the boost. |
| Fresh airborne Slide also buffers one paid landing Slide for the existing 100 ms input window, except during an active air dodge. | The same Respawn interview describes slide-hopping: air Slide, then a jump following landing, connects ground traversal moves. It documents shipped use and developer intent, not controlled retention or learning results. | Air Slide still fast-falls freely. Only real grounded admission can spend for Slide, and speed, commitment, cooldown and current Stamina are checked again. The active dodge keeps its existing fast-fall/wavedash path. Neutral Evade offers descent without a landing purchase. No held repetition or automatic jump is introduced. |

[Dodge Roll's Dave Crooks](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-the-guns-and-dungeons-of-i-enter-the-gungeon-i-) describes dodge as an effective early solution for bullet density and optional execution depth as preferable to burdening every new player. This supports preserving ordinary dodge/steering and making the extra chain a deliberate timing choice. It does not validate the new boost magnitude or prescribe a landing buffer.

Both decisions retain opponent-readable restrictions: entry follows the actual velocity instead of cursor aim, side input spends momentum, opposite input brakes before reversal, ground hazards affect the low slide, and Slide grants no dodge invulnerability. The buffer expires, newer movement intent replaces it, cancel clears it, and the following Slide Jump purchases its own premium. The momentum route therefore trades resource and a committed travel lane for speed.

The regression additions live in `test_movement_overhaul.gd`: real sprint-jump predecessors, eight headings, actual small/middle/large profiles, slow-once semantics, the global cap, post-boost carve/brake loss, repeated-input cooldown and exhaustion, actual landing payment, Slide Jump continuation, expired/empty/slow-entry/commitment/cancel/newer-intent/chord/held-repeat/active-dodge controls, and delayed authority acknowledgement replay of every prediction field. The earlier 840-unit entry expectation now includes exactly the declared one-time bounded boost; collision clipping and unchanged paid protection/cooldown assertions remain.

Falsification: removing either new behavior should fail its positive route assertions; any change to unrelated dodge conversion, stale-input rejection, exact payment, body collision or replay is a regression. A practice comparison must still determine whether the extra travel is readable and useful against the denser combat tempo. Automated checks cannot establish that feel or PvP balance.

The later flight request is already supported by the current mechanic: every character first jumps normally, while Sylph, Wyrmborn, Angel and Demon profiles can deliberately press Jump again in air and hold it for paid, finite flight. Non-flyers use that second press for their one double jump. The guide and live coach now say this explicitly. A new all-30 regression in the existing movement-overhaul suite exercises ground press/hold/release, the actual airborne second purchase, exact profile-adjusted cost, sustained height and drain, release, passive activation, and an exhausted third press through the full input-buffer expiry. Flight presentation must use `air_floating && float_ticks > 0`; the historical DOUBLE_JUMP enum alone also represents ordinary double jump and is insufficient.

[EA's current abilities guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/apex-legends/abilities/) documents Valkyrie's finite jetpack fuel and absence of fuel recharge while airborne. This is a matching shipped precedent for keeping racial flight finite and preventing wall contact or repeated Jump from resetting its opportunity. H.E.X. retains its own existing Stamina, duration and casting policies; the source does not establish those exact numbers or prove the new animation's usability.

[Respawn's 2021 mobility design note](https://www.ea.com/able/news/evolution-collection-event) identifies extreme-angle momentum preservation, limited counterplay and opaque inputs as design concerns, and explicitly values skill within movement constraints. This supports preserving turn loss, actual-velocity entry and paid finite chains. The note also records a fix for infinite Valkyrie flight. Its proposed tap-strafe change was marked delayed, so it is evidence of design reasoning and an exploit fix, not proof that tap-strafing was removed or a claim about today's exact implementation.

[STRAFTAT's official store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2386720/STRAFTAT/) establishes its fast arena-round context. Its developer-authored 1.4.9 announcement, available through [Steam's official news API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2386720&count=30&maxlength=0), explicitly repairs animation desynchronization and permanent-slide presentation. This supports checking that slide, tucked jump and flight poses follow current accepted movement state. It does not establish H.E.X.'s 90-unit boost, animation cadence or balance. Those remain candidate choices requiring native inspection and play testing.
