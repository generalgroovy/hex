## Authority and observed baseline

`MovementSystem.is_combat_intangible` admits only the opening of an accepted roll or air dodge. At120Hz, the180ms air dodge becomes22 ticks with15 protected ticks: remaining22..8 is protected and7..1 is vulnerable recovery. The240ms roll becomes29 ticks with16 protected ticks: remaining29..14 is protected and13..1 is vulnerable. These are H.E.X. values derived from its ceiling tick conversion, not another game's frame data. Movement timers advance before the current step's combat; presentation must read the accepted state immediately.

Spawn protection uses positive `spawn_protection_ticks`, which resource stepping decrements before movement/combat. Active projectile and chemistry admission explicitly rejects protected targets. `PlayerResourcesSystem.damage` itself checks dodge intangibility but relies on those callers for spawn protection, so a generic helper-only damage probe is not the complete spawn contract. `JumpPresentation.protection_ratio` gives spawn precedence, then checks the exact dodge predicate. Spawn currently reports a binary ratio1 while active, not a normalized countdown with a stored original duration.

At the preserved baseline, `CartoonChampionPresenter.protection_contract` and `_draw_evasion_contour` already had truthful expiry. Spawn used squared markers plus the native badge; timed avoidance used split diagonal strokes and two slash marks. The meaning and phase boundary survived reduced effects and were independent of the decoration budget. Flight, crouch/height clearance and the legacy jump timer did not create a shield. The baseline body draw itself was unchanged by protection.

## Reference basis and adaptation limits

- Nintendo's official [Brawl shielding and dodging guide](https://www.smashbros.com/wii/en_us/howto/basic/basic06.html), re-opened for this audit, separately teaches shields and directional dodges with temporary invincibility. This supports keeping different protection actions visually distinguishable. It does not prescribe H.E.X. geometry, shader colors, Melee frame data or specific recovery timing.
- [Dave Crooks's Enter the Gungeon design interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-the-guns-and-dungeons-of-i-enter-the-gungeon-i-), re-opened for this audit, explains that invulnerability-frame dodging supplied another response to dense bullets beyond weaving. He also describes controlling basic-input complexity. This supports making an existing paid defensive opening understandable without another input. It does not establish the precise protected fraction of Gungeon's roll, these H.E.X. timings, or measured success of a body treatment.

The proposed body treatment is an original presentation inference responding to the user's request and this native observation. Its benefit must be checked locally; the commercial success of either reference is not causal evidence.

## Implementation and review constraints

1. Derive a body treatment from the same immediate `active` and `kind` contract. Apply it to actual source-alpha body parts for ground, tuck and flight, preserving pose, source regions, face/costume identity, pixel scale and collision. A stable luminance/edge treatment can supplement the existing shapes; hue or blinking alone is insufficient. Do not add a second ghost body, rectangular backing fill or unbounded per-frame texture generation.
2. On the first vulnerable recovery tick, restore the ordinary body immediately and remove all protection marks. Do not tie this to a slowed pose key, interpolation alpha, animation timer or fading tail. A continued roll pose must not continue promising immunity. Normal and reduced motion must agree on every protected/vulnerable boundary.
3. Preserve spawn precedence while its timer is positive. When it ends, show timed-dodge treatment only if the actual dodge predicate is still true. Dead/null/config-missing states fail closed. Flight, jump, low posture, landing, wall contact and chain cues never become universal protection.
4. Keep the prior last-protected/first-vulnerable checks and add body-route checks over all30 identities, eight aim directions and both effect profiles. Include unchanged presentation tick across the boundary, spawn/dodge overlap, small compact bodies, wide flying anatomy, missing optional art fallback and zero decoration budget. Verify native protected/recovery pairs in busy ordinary play as well as the status board. Automated metadata checks alone cannot certify body contrast or subjective readability.
