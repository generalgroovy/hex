# Element and delivery readability

Status: implemented and locally qualified in `p60-6d80c3bce71e`; see [integration evidence](integration.md) for Full/native/package results. References and automation do not establish human fun, balance or sustained performance.

## Evidence and transfer

[Magicka 2's official description](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/magicka-2/about) documents a compact elemental vocabulary combined into many spells, including hybrid elements. Its shipped combination mechanic supports exposing ingredients separately from delivery. It does not establish that H.E.X.'s 63 masks, 504 choices, costs or sector drawings are balanced or readily learnable. The sector translation is a local hypothesis: each ingredient retains its established material palette and glyph rather than collapsing a mixture to one representative color.

In the [Dodge Roll developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-the-guns-and-dungeons-of-i-enter-the-gungeon-i-), Dave Crooks describes learning weapon/reload rhythm, moving mandatory active reload into an optional item after it overwhelmed new players, and making tables and other room objects matter during gunfights. This supports a readable deadline and cover response without another player input. It is not evidence for an exact 600ms Fuse or 36px Mantle radius; those are current combat rules merely projected here.

Two supplementary first-party production references sharpen the visual constraint. [Riot's VFX guide](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2017/10/dev-leagues-vfx-style-guide/) explicitly organizes clarity and clutter control around gameplay, value, color, shape and timing. [VALORANT's shader article](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/valorant-shaders-and-gameplay-clarity) describes restrained directional lighting, geometric gameplay edges, and preserving gameplay information at lower effect settings. These support bounded northwest light/southeast shading, unchanged collision marks and equal rule information in reduced effects. They document studio practice and intent, not controlled proof that this candidate improves H.E.X.

## Current implementation

- A new read-only `ElementStatusPresenter.draw(canvas, actor, screen_pos, config, language, reduced)` displays at most six stable ailment glyphs above the body. Their rules are Earth focus disruption, Fire burning, Water chill, Air gust, Light reveal and Dark wither. They use actual channel ticks and a fixed one-second bar scale. Refresh may retain earlier source attribution, so the bar never claims to measure a fraction of an original hit's duration. Empty Fire damage pools dim the burning glyph. Expired, malformed and defeated states have no badges. Open rectangular badges cannot be mistaken for the closed spawn shield or intangibility body slashes.
- `FoundationSpellPresenter.draw_expanded_projectile` handles mixed masks and Mantle/Fuse/Split/Rebound before the normal Hex projectile route. The seven projectile families remain projectile descriptors; Spray is a separate instantaneous cone. The single-element original four-family art remains on its existing route.
- Mixed projectiles use one bounded disk partitioned into ingredient sectors, with existing native flight texture details, inset material glyphs, directional light and a family mark. Physical contact ticks keep the immutable radius when the strength core shrinks. There is no new collision, trajectory, camera motion or full-screen effect.
- Mantle's open perimeter shows its current36px radius and four chips of its actual shared damage pool. Its display plane follows the supplied owner's real jump presentation; a subtle shadow stays on the floor. Owner identity is checked. This is an attack, not armor or invulnerability. It does not preview future trail contacts.
- An armed Fuse is recognized only from actual phase1. Its planted center never interpolates back toward its old flight. The110px warning boundary is clipped against world cover, while a separate32×6 segmented bar below the body empties over its actual remaining72ticks. Native review replaced the earlier obscured inset ring with this visible bar. The seed does not pretend to explode early. The warning describes the radial footprint; actor body overlap and cover still decide hits.
- Split phase0 has a fork mark; phase2 has one directional chevron per actual branch. Rebound marks only its remaining bounce count. Material sectors and all essential timing/budget cues are retained in reduced effects.
- Mixed Spray only colors already cached, physically clipped and observer-filtered spokes. `draw_expanded_cue` never generates a second cone or queries cover while drawing. Hidden-spoke sentinels stay hidden, reduced mode retains the existing three-spoke choice, and the origin brace is drawn only when that exact cached brace is visible.

## Gates and limits

The focused suite `test_element_delivery_presentation.gd` checks all63 masks ×8 families, ingredient order and disk coverage, seven projectile routes plus real prepared Spray geometry, every physical radius, read-only state, expiry, full/last Fuse countdown, real cover shadow, Mantle pool/height/owner isolation, Split phase distinction, and six statuses applied through the actual status policy. It preserves hidden-spoke and protected-hit negatives. These tests establish contracts, not aesthetic acceptance.

`capture_element_delivery_readability.gd` requires a fresh native `--output=res://.godot/element-delivery-native-NAME` and draws16 boards: all63 mixtures, eight delivery kinds, phase/budget examples and six status badges, in normal/reduced settings at1x and0.75x. They use production drawing and arranged live state, not a paid physical battle. Each image and source file is hashed, and actor/projectile immutability is checked. Root must inspect legibility, six-sector crowding, Fuse boundaries, Mantle versus protection distinction, depth coherence and the narrow zoom. Very small Rapid cores cannot promise all six glyphs are individually readable during motion; Loom and status views remain the detailed explanation. No native pass may be inferred from metadata alone.

Owned files: `src/presentation/element_status_presenter.gd` (new), `foundation_spell_presenter.gd`, `pixel_spell_effects.gd`, plus the focused suite and native capture.
