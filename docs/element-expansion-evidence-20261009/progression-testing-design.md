## Ownership and integration

The UI/progression worker adds `signature_challenge_catalog.gd`,
`signature_progression.gd`, `signature_challenge_session.gd`,
`spell_training_range.gd`, `developer_testing_session.gd`,
`optional_introduction.gd` and `spell_testing_panel.gd` plus two unit suites.
These files are new; there are no pre-existing versions to overwrite. The
parent preserves the qualified checkpoint and integrates Bootstrap, menus,
Loom admission and network boundaries. Combat owns mixture/delivery authority.
Existing SoloDuelTrial and SoloDuelRival remain unchanged by this worker.

- `MixtureCatalog` alone allocates wires. Signature assignments are explicit
  champion-ID/mask/family triples, unique across the 30 rivals and spanning all
  eight deliveries. Existing original spell IDs are not remapped.
- SignatureChallengeSession wraps the existing world-bound, monotonic trial.
  Rival casts are ordinary `SimCommand` requests with real resources,
  cooldowns, collision and effects. Mantle approaches for contact; Fuse aims
  at a point committed during its warning. A pause stops new rival commands;
  it does not remove existing motion, shots, paid startups or fields.
- A clean win requires the actual bound trial victory, positive player-owned
  damage and the actual owned defeat event. A bare health edit, duplicate
  observation, mutual defeat, departure or cheated attempt cannot award a
  signature. Developer participation permanently taints that attempt, even
  if toggled off before its terminal result.
- Progress has its own bounded version-one file. Loading is read-only. Saving
  stages and verifies exact bytes, preserves the first pre-save backup, then
  replaces atomically. Future/unknown records and invalid recovery copies
  remain untouched. A failed write keeps the earned spell for this session
  and reports that progress was not saved; retry retains the dirty record.
- Valid saved/equipped signatures imported before testing retain access.
  This is not a fabricated victory. Developer and Range loaners never enter
  that import or persistent learned records.
- Range enumerates all 504 definitions and prepares an ordinary validated
  twelve-slot request with a current-authority guard. Selection does not cast,
  refund, reset cooldowns or save. Restoration on the same actor returns its
  original positions, preserving the later of current and elapsed original
  cooldowns. Ordinary exit starts a fresh world with the player's own weave;
  the world transition skips remembering all temporary weaves.
- Developer toggles are session-only: all spells, actual damage protection,
  infinite Flux/Stamina, no cooldown and pause rival commands. Reset/refill,
  target reset and field retirement are explicit actions. No simulation time
  scaling or character grind is introduced. Network entry clears the session.
- OptionalIntroduction observes authoritative displacement, actual rising
  height, accepted `cast_started` events and resource recovery. It never
  equips, injects input, spends or writes a save. Advanced steps require the
  Range context and an actual paid mixed spell. Start/Skip stay explicit;
  world rebind is only used for the intentional transition into the Range.

## Controls and disclosure

F5 opens/skips the optional introduction; F6 opens the Range; Shift+F6 opens
signature challenges; Shift+F5 opens developer tools. Existing custom gameplay
bindings keep priority. All four are also menu actions; F7/F8/F9 settings
bindings retain their established meaning. The guide uses current binding
labels. Keyboard/controller users can focus list, delivery and utility buttons;
mouse users select the same rectangles. Range shows the actual mixture effect
and counter, warns about Fuse delay, and exposes reset, refill and clear.

## Acceptance boundary

New suites are `signature-progression` and `spell-testing-tools`. They cover
504 complete read-only loaner proposals, 30 unique unlocks, persistence failures
and exact backups, original-weave/cooldown restoration, cheat refusal/expiry,
actual paid cast/defeat events and synthetic observer boundary controls. A real
paid victory fixture exercises damage and reward admission; it does not certify
all 30 rival tactics. Root still owns engine execution, full end-to-end journeys,
native menu review, every delivery's actual paid behavior, Full and packaging.
Metadata coverage alone is not balance, useful AI, teaching success, human
acceptance or frame-performance evidence.
