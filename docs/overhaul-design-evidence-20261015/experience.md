# H.E.X. experience overhaul design — 15 October 2026

Status: analysis and design only. No game, asset, test, delivery, or canonical documentation changes are proposed as already implemented. Qualified baseline is `p61-d6d1bfb9c529`; its current-build foreground performance is unmeasured. Preserve the working build, immediate 6459 fallback, saves, source history, and evidence. Root owns any future engine session through the desktop launcher. ## Recommendation and decisions

Deliver the selected illustrated world with expressive, anatomically credible animated sprites, circular luminous spell cores, genuinely tall architecture, purposeful rooms, and a short learn-by-doing path. This is not a decision to manufacture thousands of HD sprite frames or to retain the present renderer at any cost. Choose the sprite production pipeline after a three-character, one-street, one-room comparison between hand-authored cycles, bounded skeletal 2D animation, and offline rig-baked sprite output over the same deterministic simulation.

Keep the current pixel renderer as the working fallback while the selected illustrated sprite slice is developed. It needs actual missing action/strafe/backpedal animation, not more interpolation of the same poses. Live3D characters or a full free-camera 3D game are not approved alternatives; they are a separately reviewed contingency only if the measured sprite production/residency budget proves infeasible. Offline3D rigging may author sprite frames without changing the approved runtime look. Do not conflate art style, authoring tools, runtime rendering, and simulation dimensions.

Priority order: (1) freeze visual/scale/clarity contracts and benchmark a representative slice; (2) complete the slice's body animation, spell grammar, tall obstacle/self-visibility, and one genuine interior; (3) integrate a concise first-session/learning flow; (4) expand proven content families and the roster; (5) qualify performance, accessibility, save/reopen, and delivery. Roster-wide production before the slice passes risks multiplying the current inconsistency and asset cost.

## Art direction and production economics

Use a limited material vocabulary: warm masonry/wood/parchment, cool quiet terrain, readable colored spell light, strong character face/hand/weapon silhouettes. Give districts different large shapes, masonry rhythm, vegetation, prop clusters and local color temperature, not a new noisy texture on every surface. Preserve broad quiet floor areas near combat and use detail to frame routes, entrances and places of interest. Avoid uniform giant paving grids becoming the most visible feature.

Character charm comes from posture, gaze, asymmetry and purposeful secondary motion: weight shifts while waiting, planted feet under braking, restrained cloth/tail follow-through, and distinct anticipation. Preserve mature character identity rather than automatically making everyone chibi. Author consistent light direction, shadow grounding, edge treatment and pixel/brush density across portraits, body, doors, props, UI icons and VFX. Existing portraits and approved assets are references and reusable material; do not delete them merely because a replacement is planned.

| Pipeline option | Strength | Cost/risk and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Illustrated rig-authored sprites | Fixed-camera painter control; can preserve 2D rendering and exact art direction; authored rig provides consistent anatomy before export. | Direction × action × frames creates large authoring/atlas/residency cost. Transparent overdraw and page uploads remain real. No automatic “HD is cheap” assumption. |
| Hand-authored illustrated sprite cycles | Maximum control over silhouettes, charm and native-scale strokes; current identity assets remain useful references. | Genuine action cycles and side/backward locomotion must be authored for 30 bodies. Sparse keys cannot be sold as completed smooth cycles. |
| Skeletal2D plus corrective sprite poses | Reuses painted parts/rig families and can reduce stored frame multiplication while remaining animated sprites. | Direction-specific anatomy, limb ordering, foreshortening and deformation need authored corrections. A rubbery paper-doll result fails the visual gate even if cheap. |

**Explicit frame/residency arithmetic, not measured engine memory:** current two 1024×512 RGBA8 pages per character equal 4 MiB logical texels; two manifests currently cover all 30, so full-roster capacity is 120 MiB. Production already bounds page residency;120MiB is not an observed current-resident total. A modest proposed 12 motions  × 8 directions  × 8 frames  × 128²  × 4 bytes is 48 MiB/character,144MiB for a three-character slice,1,440MiB/1.406GiB for 30;256² is 4× (576MiB for 3,5.625GiB for 30). These figures exclude atlas padding, mipmaps, CPU copies, staging, other art and engine overhead. Disk PNG compression does not remove decoded texture cost. Direction mirroring must respect asymmetric clothing/handedness, not be assumed free.

Benchmark the same recognizable characters, pose coverage, lighting and dense paid spells across the selected sprite production techniques. Technical's provisional 64 MiB character-texture envelope cannot hold even three untrimmed 48 MiB full-clip sets. At the existing 15-page admission ceiling, that illustrative set would be 720 MiB before other memory. Treat this as a pipeline feasibility constraint: compare skeletal 2D with corrective poses, trimming with fixed logical pivots, bounded atlas packing and prepared clip residency. Do not stream a missing essential pose at the moment a paid action fires. Decide using production time, memory high-water/steady/re-entry, frame-time tails and visual acceptance. Restrict current visible-roster pages/rigs, prewarm the next needed content, and verify release; do not load 30 complete motion libraries at boot.

The three-character slice should span small/medium/large and distinct anatomy (for example S.Wayne, Oh Tipi and Red Baron), with the actual camera and a tall doorway. Roster-wide expansion waits for side/backward movement, hit/defeat, casting while moving, airborne actions and contact readability to pass on these bodies. The slice is a proof, not the final limit of the requested overhaul.

## Animation state and clock contract

| Layer | Source of truth | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Simulation | Existing fixed tick, paid state, movement/cast timers, impact/defeat and network snapshots | Animation never changes cost, contact, release, position, hurtbox or an ability's authority. |
| Locomotion | Actual ground/air displacement plus aim/travel relation | Distance-driven planted contacts for walk/sprint/strafe/backpedal/wallrun. Stop without feet continuing to slide. Do not reverse a forward walk as finished backward anatomy. |
| Action pose | Actual startup/release/recovery, jump charge/vertical velocity, landing and evade windows | Visible anticipation, exact release accent, readable vulnerable recovery; preserve legal movement+cast combinations and actual interruptions. The event can outlast/shorten an art clip without delaying gameplay. |
| Presentation interpolation | Adjacent authoritative samples and bounded render interpolation | Smooth root/aim/pose transitions; no interpolation through a teleport, reset, death, room transition or hard correction. History is scoped to identity/epoch. |
| Cosmetic motion | Bounded presentation clock | Breathing, cloth, foliage and glow may vary; freeze/reduce them independently for accessibility. They do not imply invulnerability, extra reach or a moving collision core. |

Author real start/loop/stop/brake keys and turn/strafe/backward poses, then reuse rig families only where anatomy permits. Death and hard impact override incompatible casting; a new paid startup must not inherit a previous release flash. Preserve the existing precise startup clock and actual wall attachment; do not manufacture wallrun by moving the picture away from a grounded actor. Critical contact poses should remain legible at 30 Hz as well as 60/120; higher frame count is not permission to erase anticipation or add latency. Detailed state precedence belongs with Gameplay's contract and must be the same in replay, prediction and remote interpolation.

Acceptance: inspect native-size moving sequences with slow advance plus real-time playback, eight aim directions, forward/side/back travel, short interrupted casts, long casts, repeated landings, active/dead reset, and repeated menu/room transitions. Check foot/pivot drift, limb discontinuities, tail/cape clipping, facing legibility and exact event alignment. Still contact sheets support anatomy review but cannot qualify smoothness.

## Circular luminous spells without ambiguous damage

Use a compact, round, readable **contact core** for ordinary flying energy projectiles. Add a soft bounded halo outside it, a short directional tail and restrained element-specific interior behavior. Core size follows the authoritative collision contract; halo is explicitly non-damaging. A round core still needs a front/back cue visible in a frozen frame. Preserve distinct delivery grammar: Rapid cadence, Bolt travel, Heavy mass/impact, Spray fan, Rebound redirection, Split branches, Fuse countdown/detonation and Mantle owner-bound envelope. Do not turn a fan, ground footprint or personal envelope into a misleading moving ball.

The six active chemistry elements are **Water, Fire, Air, Earth, Light and Dark** (`mixture_catalog.gd` and `spell_status_policy.gd`). Differentiate them by luminance pattern, interior motif, tail rhythm and cue as well as hue: cohesive ripples for Water, ember/plasma for Fire, airy spirals for Air, a segmented mineral rim for Earth, radial ivory/gold accents for Light, and inward violet/eclipse accents for Dark. Ice belongs to Water treatment and Charge to Fire-related treatment; they are not two extra active elements. These are art proposals for review, not new mechanics or extra status promises. Mixed recipes need a dominant core and a small, stable secondary signature; avoid six simultaneous high-frequency decorations hiding trajectory. Ownership/team treatment occupies a separate consistent band/edge from element identity, with high-contrast/non-color alternatives.

Ground effects retain exact damaging footprint and actual forming/active/decaying meaning. Show source timing, direction and persistence without painting dangerous-looking empty area or harmless-looking active area. Do not visually “round out” stored whole-cell coverage. Resolve the disk/fan versus hex contract and finite-pool/entity-order questions in Gameplay's design before changing art that would imply new fairness or reach. Additional paid products can mean additional threat: never collapse them to one mark without a truthful intensity representation and native one-versus-many/staggered-expiry acceptance. The withdrawn coalescing prototype is evidence against assuming reduced commands are a win.

Normal, reduced-effects and Low detail preserve the core/boundary/phase/owner/required-warning signals; reduce halo layers, sparks and incidental movement first. Aesthetic refresh is allowed to change pixels, but authority and event traces remain exact unless separately redesigned. Test hostile/friendly/mixed owners on light/dark ground, approaching/retreating/diagonal, low-health contrast, and dense overlap; ask observers to identify the incoming path, element, ownership and safe edge before judging beauty.

## Tall walls, safe occlusion and actual interiors

Define `Hlarge` from the largest approved playable character's opaque standing body, feet to crown, at the same world scale; exclude optional halo, shadow, jump and empty atlas padding. Freeze it with a reference silhouette. PixelFlipbook's nominal 68 illustrates a 136-art-unit minimum; BodyTypeProfileCatalog's large visual template 76 would imply152. These distinct current declarations must be reconciled by the measured approved sprite, not treated as interchangeable. Meet the user's ≥2× ground-to-top vertical rise for map walls and buildings, including practice walls, rather than quietly exempting current thin cover. Any deliberate low-cover exception requires explicit user/design agreement and unmistakable separate identity. Door clearance and corridor width must also fit the largest collision body.

Separate **collision footprint**, **visual vertical extent**, **occlusion volume/policy**, **door threshold**, and optional **walkable support surface**. Expanding upward art requires correct culling, sorting, static-backdrop masking, floor contact, shadow and camera framing. It must not enlarge collision/LOS simply because a roof sprite is larger. Conversely, a solid wall cannot look like a hop-over curb. Preserve a readable footline and consistent vertical faces; no repeated cap shelves that look like stairs. Rampart appearance can be decorative, but playable rampart tops require an explicit support-height, landing, projectile, traversal, save and network contract; they are not a skin tweak.

Preserve the existing living local/spectated-body overlay. Tall foreground walls/roofs may use an authored cutaway region or bounded fade around the observer, with hysteresis to prevent flashing, only after revising the current opaque-world contract. The player body/facing/ground anchor stays legible; opponent bodies, projectiles, loot and hazards behind real LOS blockers do not become visible through this exception. If a ground marker indicates the observer beneath a roof, it conveys only self position. Do not repaint the player's spells through the wall as if they pass through it. Test front/rear/corners, jumping, wall movement, defeat/spectate transitions, narrow corridors, multiple occluders and rapid movement.

Make advertised public buildings truly enterable: threshold → controlled transition → authored room → usable service/world interaction → clear return door. Start with one continuous planar Academy room, then extend the proven pattern to the Routekeeper Lodge and Farflow Hall, then finish the advertised enterable set. Give every player-facing building an explicit enterable/closed/decorative role; this inventory must not downgrade the user's requested interiors into decorative doors. Rooms need human-scale furniture, purposeful routes, consistent windows/light, interaction anchors, a landmark and a reason to enter; do not duplicate a blank room with a menu. Keep fast menu shortcuts as accessibility/convenience paths to the same services, not a mandatory walk for routine remapping.

For the first continuous planar room, ordinary passage through its open doorway keeps the same world and ongoing paid attacks; it must not freeze the action or reset held movement merely because the actor crosses the threshold. A genuine scene transfer is a different world operation: safe doorway occupancy, explicit scene/room identity, state handoff, saved return position, failed-load recovery, network ownership and no duplicated projectiles/resources. For such transfers, preserve existing “finish your action/active spells” safety until a separately reviewed persistence policy replaces it; do not silently clear paid hazards, refill resources or let a doorway evade combat. Gameplay owns this boundary. Any discrete entry interaction must release/re-arm so it cannot become an attack inside; ordinary continuous walking needs no new input handoff. Scene prefetch can prepare assets but cannot execute room authority early. Roof removal inside is a visibility presentation; it does not create walkable upstairs space.

## Compact UI, learning and first session

Organize by immediate player task while retaining the existing actions: **Continue/World**, **Character & Weave**, **Practice & Learn**, **Play with friends**, **Settings**. Map and the current objective are one navigation view; Range, introduction, tactical lessons and challenges share a learning hub. Developer tools move behind an explicit Advanced/Developer entry; they remain available, named honestly, and excluded from earned progress. Do not put diagnostics or implementation vocabulary in ordinary play instructions.

Use one contextual primary action, a short explanation, and optional details. Character/recipe inspection is separate from equip/commit; show the real resource, delivery, effect and counter before commit. After an earned weave: “Equip this” → “Try it” → “Next lesson,” with actual save/session status. Existing corrected earned-reuse text is the seed, not a missing feature. Keep refusal reasons and save recovery actionable; never replace an error with a success toast. Match all displayed key/controller hints to current bindings and hide unavailable shortcuts while keeping menu access.

HUD priority: self state and current threat first, selected spell/readiness second, optional learning hint third. Consolidate persistent ground labels and instructional prose when their purpose is already visible in the current panel. World services use one icon/name near the door, with details on focus. The map should combine a recognizable district silhouette, current position, reachable service and selected route; retain 13 existing travel destinations and their safe-travel rules rather than propose more markers as progress. Large-text layouts reflow and paginate, and every hit rectangle remains separate at 960×540/200%, not merely visually fitting.

Proposed first-session journey (targets for observation, not current timed evidence): launch from the existing desktop front door → immediate Continue/Start with readable settings and input detection → short safe arrival and optional one-card movement lesson → one paid cast at a clear target → one resource-recovery/avoidance demonstration → enter the Academy and understand the selected weave → try one combination in the Range → see a next objective and a clear save outcome. Experienced players can skip each lesson and return later. No thirty-character or sixty-three-recipe wall before first movement. The full catalog stays reachable; contextual disclosure limits cognitive load, not player freedom.

Accessibility is a cross-cutting contract: preserve remaps, keyboard-only/controller paths, hold/release semantics and input guards; text scaling must include descriptions/refusals/tooltips, not only headings. Separate camera look-ahead, motion amplitude, flash/halo intensity, high-contrast/non-color cues and audio mix. Reduced motion cannot hide a lethal warning. Propose concise event captions/visual consequence equivalents and independently adjustable music/ambience/effects/UI only if/when those channels exist; the current local cue system does not imply an existing full music/ambience mix. Avoid mandatory rapid tapping and require a safe remapping/recovery path.

Audio direction: readable soft elemental identity, stronger paid contact/damage/result hierarchy, restrained room ambience and material footsteps. Preserve the current bounded voice/queue model and no hidden-source positional leak. Real listening comparisons on speakers/headphones at low volume are required; Dummy/backend tests prove lifecycle only. Existing finite result tails may finish in an otherwise eligible terminal freeze, while focus/modal/mute/reset still stop them. Any spatial ambience is cosmetic; hostile spatial cues require the same allowed visibility/source policy as the visuals.

Plug-and-play means a coherent opening screen, no missing-asset flashes, safe defaults, real controller disconnect/reconnect, readable failed-save/load recovery, predictable Alt-Tab/mute and clean restart. The existing qualified unsigned local package is valuable evidence, not a public installer or verified compatibility on every machine. Use the desktop launcher for every eventual session, preserve old working builds and profile bytes, and distinguish package/startup, input, human-feel and performance acceptance.

Exact Full/package/terrain/lifecycle review and front-door user session remain separate. |

Do not revive withdrawn prefix/style/membership/sample-budget/decoration caches by renaming them. The new design can simplify representation after threat/readability review, but needs a meaningful whole-workload performance gate. Retain a coherent working renderer until the replacement slice qualifies; migrate one content family at a time and remove superseded production paths only after reference/inventory and fallback decisions. No cleanup or art generation is authorized by this planning document.

## Primary precedents and limits

- [Riot: Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) supports hierarchy, recognizable silhouettes, truthful ability boundaries and readable projectile direction. Applied here as a design criterion, not proof that a particular HEX effect is readable or fast.
- [Nintendo: Link's Awakening graphic direction](https://zelda.nintendo.com/links-awakening/blog/director-yoshiki-haruhana/) describes a coherent miniature/diorama visual premise. Borrow consistency of scale/material/detail, not its character proportions or an unverified rendering technique.
- [Nintendo: Echoes of Wisdom, developer chapter1](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/ask-the-developer-vol-13-the-legend-of-zelda-echoes-of-wisdom-chapter-1/) separates top-down feel from behind-character3D, discusses the earlier game's 3D backgrounds/lighting, and stresses trying gameplay. This supports evaluating perspective independently from simulation and proving a slice.
- [Nintendo: Link's Awakening official game description](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-games/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Link-s-Awakening-1514327.html?nsuid=70010000020034) presents rooms as places for puzzles/traps/enemies. The proposed purposeful HEX interiors are an inference inspired by that approach; no claim about Nintendo's roof/occlusion code is made.
- [Supergiant: Inside Hades — 3D Modeling & Rigging](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYJ6d1ifSqA) is an official production account of translating hand-painted character design into models/rigs. It motivates testing a rig-authored pipeline; it does not establish HEX's asset budget or a measured 3D performance advantage.
