# Named-cast source and extraction audit

Date: 2026-09-27. Scope: the **30 current named identities**, not 189 generic race/size/presentation families. The user requested full-character proportions with size expressed principally through volume. Later clarification explicitly rejects replacing full-body artwork with detailed heads on shared geometric bodies.

## Result and boundary

Thirty generated full-body turnaround sources have measured, hash-pinned eight-facing recipes. They are **standing/idle directional candidates only**. Source extraction does not establish walk, run, slide, roll, jump, cast, wallrun, hit or independent movement/aim animation coverage. No engine was run by this audit worker and no live game art was replaced.

## Measurement and extraction

- S. Wayne skin-tone correction: only his recipe selects row2 of bodies-01-s-wayne-v2.png (SHA2566063cb5ee25d95a9d7b1e6c7fc39f037c0cbf0c102c243535c9515da0f692344). All8 facings were independently remeasured; front calibration is176 sourcepixels. Oh Tipi, Red Baron, Steezo and Treevor continue consuming unchanged bodies-01.png. Previous output remains historical review material.
- Original sources: art_batches/character_cast_v1/reference/bodies-01.png through bodies-06.png; all1536x1024 RGBA with original alpha0..254.
- Extraction threshold: existing alpha >=127 becomes visible; there is **no color-key removal, synthesized body pixel or per-facing rescale**.
- Sheets01/03/05/06 have usable transparent row/column valleys. Actual silhouettes, not nominal192x205 cells, define crops.
- Sheets02/04 have unequal row extents. Forty independent full-body alpha components are measured using horizontal-span connectivity; tiny detached source clusters are associated with the nearest figure. Transparent crop borders are required.
- All8 directions of one identity use one front crown-to-sole calibration. Crown/horn inclusion can affect perceived body height and remains a review concern. Target size heights are supplied by the builder, not encoded as per-pose fitting.
- Feet are registered from the midpoint of the bottom3.5% occupied band; renderer owns bounded nearest-neighbor rounding correction. Review tails/skirts/wing tips near the feet before promotion.
- The helper measure_sources.py is read-only and emits candidate JSON. Saved recipes preserve exact source SHA256 and opaque bounds/pixel counts for each facing.

## Visual acceptance still required

Generated directional labels are intended yaw views, not proof of exact0/45/90/135/180/225/270/315-degree anatomy. Some front quarters/rear quarters may resemble their neighbors. Check identity consistency, line of sight, silhouette volume, feet contact, limb width, apparent head/body ratio and thin wing readability at native gameplay resolution. Do not automatically crop or stretch a tall wing/crown to fit a desired body measurement.

A row of standing sprites is **not** a walking animation. The next authored-action pass must keep the accepted full-body proportions and racial anatomy rather than falling back to a mechanically unrelated torso/limbs. Hurtboxes, stamina, wall clearance, speed, networking and gameplay remain outside this art audit.
