## Findings that change the design decision

1. **Most proposed rows are redesigns.** Steam310 is sustained transparent exposure with a finite height ceiling, heat/cool channels and exit decay; Vaporwave is an expanding one-hit displacement attack. Flood316 is directional flow, while proposed Whirlpool rotates. Vortex322 currently rotates, while proposed Tornado lifts. These change matchups and movement, not spelling.
2. **The optical roles are reassigned.** Current Crystal Prism307 reflects; Crystal Lens329 splits Light; Mirrorwater320 records disturbance/reveal and projectile ripple cues; Lightbend325 rotates eligible Light projectiles. The proposal transfers reflection to 320, splitting to 307, and focusing to 329. A new name must not silently transfer semantics between serialized IDs. Saved identity, source provenance and replay versioning must remain explicit.
3. **Two proposed mirrors replace established traversal tools.** `glacier327` displays as Frostway and reduces ordinary ground target speed and physical projectile travel. `overload331` displays as Capacitor and increases ordinary ground target speed, with terminal hostile displacement. Icewall and destructible Overload are new cover/objective systems, not restored catalog behavior. Keep existing runtime until a deliberate replacement decision is approved.
4. **Allegiance is already nonuniform.** Root Fire/Steam pressure and Conflagration damage all eligible unprotected actors; generic product damage is hostile-only. Ground flow/ordinary movement modifiers often affect all teams. A single blanket friendly-fire rule would alter current contracts. The recommendation below states all three relationships explicitly.
5. **Material conservation is not the same thing as an exhaustible damage pool.** Existing products have paid quantity and finite phase clocks, while many effects compute pulses from fixed coefficients without debiting a separate damage reserve. Proposed reserved echoes, captured Charge, marks and transform counts need explicit accounting. Do not claim `quantity` alone implements all new budgets.
6. **Current conduction is not the proposed graph.** `_build_links` picks nearby live deposited sources and retiled capsule coverage; losing any selected link disables the relevant damaging path. That does not implement independently isolated wet-cell branches or an ordered breakable frozen track. Target-to-target Plasma jumps are a third, distinct topology.
7. **Cover and rings require real topology.** Permafrost305 currently blocks projectiles/sight but allows actor passage. Icewall would add segmented actor-solid geometry. Current Penumbra335 uses disk coverage despite its boundary description; a hollow Eclipse ring requires an authoritative selected-cell ring with a genuinely safe center. Small rings can quantize away their opening, so minimum viable geometry must be measured at each grid size.
8. **Pure utility remains useful.** Compulsory damage risks turning every travel aid into another hazard, makes optical setup less readable, and duplicates area-denial choices. Recommended explicit exceptions: Quicksand, Monsoon, Lightbend and Frost Lens may have no independent actor damage, with attack value supplied by paid follow-up/projectile transformation. Stone Pillar may also remain utility cover if safe damaging formation is not readable. These are requests to amend the proposal's every-reaction-attacks rule, not assumed approval. All other rows below retain a distinct proposed attack; an attack-compliant alternative is stated for each exception.

## Shared proposed contract (applies to every decision-table row)

These policies make each row explicit without inventing 36 status engines. They describe the candidate design, not implemented behavior. Numerical strengths, durations, heights, widths, pulse counts (including the attachment's suggested three hail volleys), conversion rates and caps remain unapproved.

### Phases, ownership and contact

**F → A → X:** formation reserves capacity and shows the actual selected cells, movement/strike direction and safe exits; formation is harmless until its announced activation boundary. A formation eruption is the first active event. Active state spends only admitted budgets. Expiry/decay has a distinct harmless cue, releases structure/links/marks and never refreshes the clock. Discrete strikes lock their selected cells before activation; moving fronts announce their direction and stop at solid obstacles/domain bounds. Each row's budget column supplies its active transition. Delayed traps show both trigger eligibility and time remaining. Normal/reduced effects show identical dangerous cells and phases.

Every origin retains stable recipe ID, original paid source IDs, creator/team and exact continuous contact. Pair order is symmetric; mixed-team input ownership follows the current deterministic source-order policy pending any separate approval. All new damage, harmful movement and status applications respect existing health, spawn protection, jump/air-dodge intangibility and Float/height rules. No lift grants free jump, restores an air action, cancels paid action cost, or resets protection. Physical cover blocks all allegiances. Existing Frostway/Capacitor universal traversal modifiers are retained while those redesigns are deferred.

**Allegiance codes in the direct-attack column:**

- **H:** self/allies take no reaction health damage; enemies may take budgeted damage. Secondary harmful status is enemy-only unless the row explicitly says `M`.
- **A:** self/allies/enemies all take budgeted health damage, respecting protection. Used for Steam/Vaporwave and Conflagration to retain the established misuse risk.
- **U:** no independent reaction health damage for any allegiance; proposed utility exception. Attack-compliant fallback is H unless otherwise stated.
- **M:** the specified route/traction/displacement effect affects self/allies/enemies equally while eligible and unprotected; it is a legible environmental affordance, not teammate-targeted control. Row-specific H harmful statuses remain separate. No row changes input direction.
- **C:** cleansing may affect self/allies, never enemies; one eligible mark, not a general immunity or health/Flux refund.

All unmentioned movement/projectile effects are **none**. All unmentioned cover interaction is **ordinary obstruction**, with no through-wall targeting, creation or damage. No new row creates concealment or through-wall reveal. Existing concealment rows remain unchanged until their explicit replacements are approved. Proposed concealment removal affects material only; seeing the actor still requires the unified 360-degree obstruction predicate.

### Budgets and deterministic ordering

**B** is the common finite-accounting policy: original paid quantity and remaining strength are partitioned, never copied. At admission derive explicit typed limits for damage `D`, displacement/steering `M`, interception `I`, and secondary work `S`; units are distinct and cannot be casually added. A recipe's approved allocation/conversion schedule binds them to paid inputs. Every pulse/target/branch debits its assigned share, unused shares expire, and absence of capacity refuses before spending. No unapproved numeric conversion is supplied here.

An origin has a fixed expiry and bounded selected-cell set, contact set, phase schedule and event allowance. Damage caps cover all targets together, with stable ordering when several actors compete for the last budget. Per-origin/per-pulse actor deduplication is mandatory; crossing multiple cells cannot multiply one hit, and leaving/re-entering cannot refill a reserve. Repeated displacement has both a total displacement/steering allowance and a recovery/reattachment gate. Opposing effects use a bounded shared resolver rather than alternately moving an actor without limit. Dead owner, disconnect, expiry, simultaneous break and reset all have deterministic cleanup. No product/product recursion or automatic re-reaction is introduced.

For pooled scarce budgets the proposed order is phase/time-of-contact, then stable effect ID, then actor/projectile ID, with walls winning existing contact ties. Whether the player accepts the fairness of a shared actor-damage pool is a design question; do not silently change current fixed-per-actor pulse damage to this scheme.

### Projectile transform policy T

Proposed eligible transforms begin with physical Bolt/Heavy/Rapid projectiles; instantaneous Spray and reaction-generated pulses/echoes are excluded unless separately approved. Keep Light-only eligibility for Lightbend/Frost Lens in the first optical prototype; broadening to all elements is a separate design decision. Transformation applies symmetrically to self/allied/enemy projectiles. Preserve original owner/team, source-cast identity, damage credit and hostility after reflection/redirection; therefore reflecting an enemy shot does **not** make it friendly to the reflector. Any future ownership transfer requires a separate explicit contract and source/kill-credit treatment.

Keep original absolute expiry, remaining range and source ledger. Reflection/redirection preserves speed, width and damage unless a row explicitly says otherwise. Splitting partitions damage **and** material/branch allocation, carries hit dedupe and transforms-used state, and reserves child capacity before changing the parent. Focusing may trade width for speed but adds no damage, range or lifetime; acceleration must still pass swept contacts and reaction-time review. An origin's one transform charge is finite; a projectile's ancestry carries a bounded transform allowance across all optics so reflection chains cannot circulate forever. No branch can split recursively. A failed split/transform preserves the original projectile. Continue contacts from the exact hit point, rechecking walls and other events in the remaining tick; never teleport to the far side of cover. New transform budgets/state require snapshot/replay support even when existing velocity/branch plumbing is reusable.
