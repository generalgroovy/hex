## 1. Make Gust honor the existing push-resistance contract

**Priority: P1. Classification: confirmed consumer/description inconsistency; ordinary-paid regression pending.**

`src/sim/combat/spell_status_policy.gd:43` applies Air's first-entry Gust with an unconditional `direction * 80` to both ground and air velocity. In contrast, `src/sim/movement/movement_system.gd:402` scales forced-control speed through `knockback_ratio`. The shared catalog calls that effect **push taken** (`src/content/character_perk_catalog.gd:48`). Dwarf, Minotaur and Stoneborn advertise resistance in crouch, sprint and jumpsquat respectively (`src/content/race_perk_catalog.gd:37`, `:79`, `:100`); Treevor, Juul and Joh describe reduced or increased displacement in their active windows (`src/content/character_perk_catalog.gd:19`, `:37`, `:39`). No displayed contract excludes Gust. Gust itself is explicitly described as a push (`src/content/spell_status_catalog.gd:10`).

Existing elemental tests verify the unmodified 80 px/s push and no second shove on refresh (`tests/unit/test_elemental_spell_expansion.gd:183`, `:186`). The prepared perk diagnostics exercise knockback through `LAUNCHED` only (`scripts/balance-audit-perks.gd:105`). Thus both suites can pass while the advertised modifier has no effect on this common elemental push.

**Hypothesis and smallest change.** At Gust entry, evaluate the existing bounded perk ratio once against the target's pre-impulse state, scale the 80,000-unit impulse, and use that same result for both vector components and both velocity stores. Evaluating after the first velocity write would incorrectly end Treevor's stationary window halfway through one effect. Preserve real paid damage as admission, status expiry and attribution, the first-entry rule, protections, clamping, and the absence of stun or a launch state. Do not turn Gust into forced control or change authored ratios.

**Subtasks and acceptance:**

1. Independently review the catalog/policy contract before changing production. Inventory every current `knockback_ratio` row rather than testing Treevor alone.
2. Add explicit prepared active/inactive boundary checks for all three ancestry and three personal modifiers, plus an unperked control. These fixtures establish consumer arithmetic, not the natural frequency of obtaining each window. Include diagonal direction and refresh invariance; compare impulse deltas rather than whole existing velocity.
3. Add a real paid Air-contact comparison through `SimWorld`: ordinary cast admission/release, positive owned direct loss, status entry, and the expected scaled displacement versus a matched neutral control. Do not edit resources, timers, flight or status lanes during the run. Keep existing protection and source-conservation checks.
4. Run the elemental and perk suites under root's serialized gate. A success establishes that a documented push modifier reaches Gust without changing damage, payment or protection. It does **not** establish human reaction fairness, equal roster strength, or all six active windows occurring naturally in combat.

**Established-game precedent and transfer limit.** Respawn's [Marked midseason developer notes](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/marked-midseason-patch-notes) explain reduced displacement/stun as a way to give the affected player more agency.
