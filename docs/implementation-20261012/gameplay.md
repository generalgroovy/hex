## Protected-contact integration correction

The joined-route inspection found a real integration gap: `OptionalIntroduction` waits for a fresh protected projectile/spray/beam crossing, but the whole-map Hex projectile resolver filtered intangible/high actors before the swept contact query. Legacy projectile combat already records `evaded_projectile`; the Hex path could not provide that witness.

The candidate queues a protected hostile contact at the same swept time and actor priority, then observes it only after earlier walls, product deflections, clashes, fields and target termination have been resolved. It writes the existing `last_event` only, before projectile-center or Fuse changes. It does not spend strength, damage, intercept or redirect the shot. The dedicated regression compares ordinary paid Light Bolt flight against grounded, actual paid jump, covered jump and geometric-miss controls, and requires the protected passed shot to retain its strength. A second paired fixture uses ordinary paid jump/double-jump inputs to retain height through the entire slow-shot passage; every per-tick Bolt/Fuse projectile canonical field and owned ledger matches a same-position friendly-actor control, and Fuse arms only at its intended later landing. Root reported hex-avoidance-feedback: 1,899 assertions passing with empty stderr, repeated successfully in the clean Full. Technical independent review found no hidden projectile mutation or ordering defect. Because `last_event` is authoritative state, the final Full deterministic/collision checks were retained and passed rather than relying only on the narrow observer regression.

## Primary precedents and adaptation limits

- [Respawn's Titanfall controls interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designer-interview-getting-i-titanfall-i-s-controls-just-right) supports testing traversal against real geometry, readable wallrun anticipation and purposeful momentum choices. H.E.X. finite costs and the three routes come from its own live policies; this precedent does not validate their numerical balance.
- [Dodge Roll's Enter the Gungeon developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-the-guns-and-dungeons-of-i-enter-the-gungeon-i-) supports authored encounter rooms, repeated testing and deliberate firing rhythms. H.E.X. adapts readable pressure/recovery decisions; three 18-tick bursts and its eight delivery counters need their own encounter and player testing.
- [Capcom's Mega Man 11 launch explanation](https://news.capcomusa.com/mega_man/blog/2018/10/02/ready-mega-man-11-is-out-now) supports learning a defeated boss's weapon and reusing it against another opponent. It motivates the existing earned-signature loop, not arbitrary unlock grind or a claim that three lessons constitute a campaign.
- [Paradox's Magicka2 description](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/magicka-2/about) supports composition and experimentation as a combat premise. It does not prove all 63 H.E.X. recipes are equally useful or mechanically distinct; the new matrix exposes shared mechanics instead of assuming uniqueness.
- [Respawn's Arsenal Range update](https://www.ea.com/ea-play/news/arsenal-season-game-updates) supports configurable practice-target behavior. H.E.X. implements only bounded ordinary commands and explicit measurement controls, with finite resources and no earned rewards.
