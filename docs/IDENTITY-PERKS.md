# Identity perks: player field guide

Status: **current authored passive rules, reviewed October 12, 2026**. The 21 ancestry and 30 personal identities retain their existing conditions and percentages. The gameplay audit makes paired spell timing respect actual whole-tick outcomes, as explained below. The follow-up exercises all 51 through prepared legal trigger fixtures and real consumers; this does not establish natural activation frequency, isolated causal benefit or human balance. The [implementation record](implementation-20261012/gameplay.md) reports that boundary. The [specification](../SPECIFICATION.md#race-and-character-passives) owns the contract and the [resume](../.agent/HEX-RESUME.md) owns current qualification. The [October 9 research record](REFERENCE-IDENTITY-REFINEMENT.md) explains the original decisions; it is historical evidence.

Every character has one ancestry perk and one personal perk. Existing movement, aim, casting and resource states activate them automatically; there is no extra ability button. The Gallery overview names both perks. Gallery Details and the Compendium show their full activation windows and limitations. Displayed resource and movement stats remain the base profile.

Percentages below modify the named quantity. Less damage, knockback, movement cost, startup time or spell cooldown is beneficial. More regeneration, acceleration or steering strengthens that quantity. Stronger braking sheds speed faster; weaker braking carries momentum farther. These are conditional adjustments, not new protection, resource refills, additional dodges or flight resets.

**Timing matters.** Startup reads your state before paying Flux. A spell's cooldown reads it again when the paid startup releases. Spending or moving between those moments can activate one side of a perk without the other. A spell keeps its own cooldown when moved to another slot; its cooldown does not lock other ready spells. The existing post-cast recovery timer remains a trigger for some movement/defensive perks, not a new global casting lock.

**Paired timing needs two real changes.** Charged Focus, Last Air Gambit, Scrap Spark, Low Bellows and Set the Anvil pair startup and cooldown adjustments. For each current window, both must move that spell's actual whole-tick timers in the advertised directions; otherwise neither timing factor from that perk applies. For example, Rapid's one-tick startup cannot become faster, so a paired faster-startup/longer-cooldown perk supplies neither an impossible faster start nor a real repeat penalty. The opposite bargain cannot gain a shorter cooldown when its slower startup rounds away. Other kinds of tradeoff, such as steering versus cooldown, keep their existing rules.

The [shared timing policy](../src/sim/entities/identity_perk_policy.gd) checks ancestry first, then the personal modifier, including accepted stacking and current startup status. Inspection reports the current effective ticks and any suppressed pairs. Admission and release still sample separately, so a current-window preview does not promise that moving or spending before release will leave its cooldown unchanged.

Health, Flux and Stamina recovery keeps its normal waits, action restrictions and chemical suppression. Ordinary recovery ramps also remain. A recovery perk cannot heal through an active delay or make an airborne phase regenerate Stamina. Ground walking means ordinary motion outside sprint, crouch and authored movement; falling and rising exclude held flight and wallrun. High Flux/Stamina means at least two-thirds full, high Health at least three-quarters, and a low resource at most one-third.

When both perks modify the same quantity in the same window, their factors multiply. The combined perk factor is clamped to 75–135% of the otherwise applicable value. Positive paid actions still have a positive cost; reductions never create immunity.

- **Dr. Apex, grounded jump preparation with high Stamina:** Stoneborn Gathering Weight and Counterweight multiply damage received by 0.95 × 0.90 = 0.855 before fixed-point rounding. Knockback is 15% lower. Their braking factors multiply to 1.20 × 0.90 = 1.08, so held preparation still sheds momentum 8% faster than the base action. Counterweight reduces the ancestry's extra drag; it does not erase it.
- **Biggy Bob, crouched with low Flux:** Dwarf Low Bastion supplies 7% less damage, 15% less knockback and 10% less ground speed. When both timers can change, Low Bellows shortens startup 15%; its 15% longer cooldown applies if that spell releases while Biggy remains crouched and low on Flux. A Rapid spell suppresses this paired timing adjustment rather than applying only its drawback.
- **Faab I Yaina:** paid held flight uses Angel Poised Wings. A healthy descent instead uses Steady Descent. Those windows do not overlap: releasing flight changes which passive can apply.

The tables preserve the current source descriptions. Race rows apply to every character of that ancestry; character rows add the personal rule.
