# Rules 12 systems audit

Independent source audit by the systems specialist, followed by bounded implementation. The audit inspected rules 11 before editing: the simultaneous resolver, spell access, story profile and encounters, map generator, planning interface, battlefield renderer, public-state bots, feedback and audio. No browser observation or human enjoyment study is implied by these source findings.

The strongest foundation is deterministic simultaneous resolution, persistent story progression, seeded mirrored terrain, and consistent effective-spell previews. The principal structural weaknesses were contained-pair stacking, unrestricted between-turn loadout replacement, and repeated battle explanations competing with the board. Rules 12 addresses those foundations while retaining historical engines.

## Implementation priorities and resulting contract

1. **Exact combinations:** `combinations-v12.ts` defines all 63 nonempty subsets (6 singles, 15 pairs, 20 triples, 15 four-element, 6 five-element, 1 all-element). Each has a stable ID, mask, short rule, explicit mechanics and generated full description. Every one has a distinct mechanical fingerprint. Magma is slow hazardous ground, Quagmire leaves Wet, and Solar Flare grants a little Flux as well as Clarity, distinguishing those pairs from their single-element parents.
2. **Public story inhabitants:** `inhabitants-v12.ts` supplies deterministic intentions. A pulse joins the same batch as player casts. An announced counter element hitting its perch suppresses that pulse and still consumes ordinary spell resources. NPCs remain separate from champion units.
3. **Fixed kits and smaller planning burden:** rules 12 rejects nonempty in-match loadout and affinity preparations. Camp choices persist through the match. Historical behavior remains in the historical engines.
4. **Living presentation:** the renderer specialist owns connected terrain, creatures and readable previews. Exact-field interfaces prevent visual labels from inventing different rules.
5. **Integration evidence:** exhaustive mask tests and representative interaction tests precede transport, browser, phone and human checks. Each layer establishes a different claim.

### Shared fields

`CombinationMechanics` uses bounded existing concepts: movement (`flood`, `rough`, `ice`, `current`), aggregate spell mitigation, cover, persistent/formation/arrival hazards, healing, Flux recovery, existing statuses, cleansing, well income, and one-exchange dispersal. Higher mixtures replace contained pairs. For example, Fire + Water + Earth is Hot Spring: slow protective healing ground, with neither hidden Steam mitigation nor hidden Magma damage. All six make Worldseed: one exchange of shelter, cleansing and Flux recovery, followed by dispersal.

`fieldCombination(cell)` returns the live exact phenomenon; `fieldEffects(cell)` returns its mechanics; `previewField(cell, incomingElements, freeze)` forms a cloned result including natural Earth or Water substrate. These functions are shared with consumers. Natural biome properties such as forest cover and marsh entry remain geography, distinct from temporary elemental inheritance.

### Timing and limits

All casts read the same post-movement snapshot. Inhabitant counters are checked against that accepted cast batch. The union is computed before direct damage, healing and statuses. Displacement follows. The final cell determines environmental hazard damage, capped at one. Wells score before transient fields expire. Worldseed therefore still permits ordinary well scoring but cannot silently inherit Penumbra. Guard, Ward, Resolve, Wither and other existing protections retain their timing.

Creature intentions are derived from canonical species, fixed anchors and round; no hidden mutable phase enters the save. `inhabitant` events carry an explicit `interrupted` boolean so presentation does not parse prose. Restore validates species, anchor, elements, combination ID and event kind. The rules-12 event bound is 600, accommodating six large spell footprints plus three bounded creature footprints and unit feedback; the visible rolling log remains bounded to 150.

## Source verification

`tests/combinations-v12.test.ts` covers catalogue coverage/uniqueness, substrate and ordering, explicit movement/ice/cover, all 63 descriptors executed in a seeded match, no pair leakage, hazard timing, cleanse and anti-lock behavior, all-six simultaneous resolution and dispersal, fixed kits with rules-11 preservation, strict restore rejection, real creature releases and paid counters, visible-lethal-field AI avoidance, and complete deterministic AI matches. The world specialist separately covers all species and encounter placements. Integration-owner checks cover transport, rendering and deployment.

This document records the intended evidence chain. A passing automated test does not establish physical-phone feel, player comprehension, competitive fairness or commercial success.

### Bounded AI counterplay follow-up

The AI counter improvement adds no search dimension. It scores only already-enumerated, legal spell targets whose footprint reaches an announced counter perch. The score compares complete fields with and without the interrupted releases, including surviving natural substrate, existing chemistry and earlier allied casts. The counter spell still deposits its own element. Lost benefits to other allies and benefits given to enemies count against the choice. Existing Flux cost and friendly-fire rejection remain in force, and the additional counter value is capped. An already planned interruption removes that pulse from subsequent candidates, preventing duplicate rewards.

`tests/ai-inhabitants-v12.test.ts` verifies: a paid empty-perch counter saves a rooted ally outside the spell footprint; no threatened ally produces no cosmetic interruption; insufficient Flux and friendly fire remain blocking; and two available casters plan only one counter. This establishes deliberate public-information counterplay, not optimal or human-like strategic play.

Final targeted verification passed **30 tests** across `ai-inhabitants-v12.test.ts`, `combinations-v12.test.ts`, and `inhabitants-v12.test.ts`. This includes a frost-presentation regression covering static spell records, lookup and affinity-scaled descriptions: rules 12 says unmixed Water, combat values stay unchanged, and rules-11 wording is preserved. These are targeted systems/story results, separate from the integration owner's full-suite, browser and deployment evidence.
