Four independent specialists reviewed every area below: `design_audit_systems`,
`design_audit_experience`, `design_audit_world`, and `design_audit_validation`.
The first three proposed changes independently; the fourth challenged the combined
proposal against the actual rules, transport, progression and input contracts.
Implementation is shared by systems, world, experience and the integrating agent.
Cross-review and final evidence are recorded separately from these design hypotheses.

## Shared contracts

- `combinations-v12.ts` is the authority for all exact elemental identities and
  bounded mechanics. The complete set includes the natural ground, existing field,
  legal player casts and un-interrupted public creature pulses.
- One field resolves per hex. Singles have no reaction ID; compounds have exactly
  one. Higher mixtures do not silently inherit Flood, Rough ground or Current.
- Inhabitants are story-only neutral beings. They announce a bounded footprint,
  alternate release and rest, and can be interrupted by a named element on their
  perch. They do not block, score, respawn as champions or award progression.
- One inhabitant appears in the first three encounters, two in the next 21, and
  three in the last six: 63 authored appearances. Existing boss rewards remain.
- Existing rules 11 saves and rooms keep their old engine and route. Only transport
  handoff is updated so an explicitly requested rematch can enter current rules.
- Imported H.E.X. environment files are copied intact with hashes/provenance.
  Decoration has no authority over movement, cover or collision.

## Fourth review: reliability challenges retained

The validation specialist independently inspected all 24 areas and confirmed the
architecture, with these required corrections: natural forest/ruin Earth and marsh
Water must appear in previews; fixed kits must be enforced by engine, AI and wire,
not merely hidden in UI; creature contributions must enter one simultaneous batch;
affinity totals are not equivalent power; old tests stay attached to old engines;
phone acceptance measures usable board height and interaction, not just overflow.

## Limits of acceptance

Passing deterministic tests does not establish fun, visual charm, accessibility
for every player, competitive balance, physical-phone comfort or market demand.
Those claims require observed human play and real device/listening sessions.
The release evidence must distinguish implemented behavior, automated checks,
browser emulation, published verification and outstanding human evaluation.

## Integrated candidate evidence — 2026-10-18

- Four specialists completed separate initial and implementation reviews of all
  24 areas. The systems interruption logic also received independent review from
  the validation specialist after implementation.
- Complete automated suite: **539 passed, zero failed**. This includes exact
  execution of all 63 fields, all 30 Story encounters, fixed kits, paid creature
  counters, legacy engines and pending-room compatibility.
- TypeScript check passed. The final mobile toolbar correction was followed by
  another successful typecheck and production build.
- Production Worker with a fresh local D1 database: **244 checks across 60 HTTP
  requests**. A complete 20-round mirror match ended 30–30, then rematched. Checks
  covered sealed orders, retry recovery, two-seat agreement, immutable kits,
  rejected forged NPC/training input and competitive affinity matching.
- Browser play: selected all three champions, planned movement, undid and
  reapplied an order, guarded, cast two intersecting spells, and resolved two
  exchanges. Round three was 2–2 as predicted by the same public engine. Creature
  inspection exposed the announced footprint and counter; Replay and Fit Spell
  worked; a seven-hex Tidal Surge could be queued and undone.
- Browser viewports: 1366×900, 390×844 and 320×568 CSS pixels, without horizontal
  page overflow. At 390×844 the board is 287px high and End Turn remains within
  the viewport. At 320×568 vertical scrolling is needed; the command row remains
  sticky within its tray. Six camera controls and creature inspection occupy
  intentional separate rows, with 44px touch targets. No browser console errors
  were observed in this journey.
- Six equal-affinity AI diagnostics across three seeds and swapped seating
  produced the same team result in both seating orders. Matches lasted 8–12
  rounds; scoring continued after the opening approach. This small deterministic
  sample is not a population-level balance study.
- Environment atlases retain their original bytes and provenance. Creatures
  use 13 animated body families with authored elemental details; these are not
  63 independently drawn sprite sheets. Audio scheduling, voice limits and
  lifecycle are tested; listening quality and physical-phone performance remain
  unverified.

Publication identity and live HTTP evidence are recorded by the integrating
agent after saving and deploying this exact candidate. Human novice observation,
repeat-play preference and competitive matchup testing remain the evidence needed
to make stronger claims about ease of learning, enjoyment and balance.
