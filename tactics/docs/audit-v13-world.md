# Confluence design audit: world, identity and learning

Independent review of the released rules-12 source at `eb572976914cf3024154b4c1039b5c6965e6b4b8`, before implementation. All 24 areas were considered. This review emphasizes the experience of discovering a company, learning a landscape and returning for another match. It does not infer enjoyment from catalogue size or automated correctness.

## Raw material and strongest existing qualities

Inspected the actual current engine, spell and affinity functions, story generation and rewards, all 63 inhabitant records, all exact field definitions, seeded map generation, company/story/training components, action warnings, battlefield controls, audio voices and existing tests. Prior audit prose was used to locate intended contracts, then checked against code.

The strongest foundation is a finite, shared tactical language: three champions commit movement and one spell or Guard; the landscape is public, enemy orders are hidden; four chosen spells plus a creator signature give bounded choices. Canonical H.E.X. names, affinities and artwork give the cast continuity. Deterministic maps and public creatures support repeatable observation and fair learning. Keep those qualities.

## Findings and decisions across all 24 areas

| Area | Existing strength | Weakness and source evidence | Retain / change / defer | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core identity | Simultaneous ground-targeted actions create prediction and coordination. | `spellAccess` allows every level-one spell at zero affinity, so all champions can cheaply approximate every ingredient; identity depends mostly on discounts. | Change current rules to require affinity in the spell element; retain three native elements at most and four spells plus signature. | No zero-affinity spell is legal; every starter kit works at canonical affinity; saved legal choices survive repair. |
| Turn structure | One commitment per team with deterministic move/cast/aftermath resolution. | Ward and Anchor start next round while Clarity starts immediately; current general help implies a simpler timing model than the actual engine. | Retain phases, expose precise timing only where it affects the chosen action. Update Guard advice if resistance becomes one hex. | Story advice, spell descriptions, previews and actual resolution agree on timing and displacement. |
| Tactical depth | Wells, ground targeting, friendly fire, terrain and displacement provide positional choices. | Complete Guard immunity makes high-displacement spells fail against routine well sitting, reducing differences between push magnitudes. | Support bounded Guard resistance and keep Anchored as full protection; do not add another action phase. | A two-hex push can challenge Guard, a one-hex push cannot, and timing/terrain still give defensive choices. |
| Element personalities | Six clear field personalities and exact mixtures share bounded rules. | Sixty-three unique mechanic fingerprints are coverage, not 63 memorable experiences; descriptions can demand recall. | Retain exact combinations; teach a useful interaction in context and show consequences rather than encourage collecting every recipe. | Each encounter lesson references a real verb the player can use; no lesson asks for a larger mixture merely because it is larger. |
| Spells | Forty-eight ordinary spells plus 30 signatures already cover footprints and tactical verbs. | `starterSpellIds` is a generic primary/branch/secondary recipe; 30 characters produce only 24 different kits, with several identical trios/pairs. | Author 30 legal native kits with a clear role, meaningful support or control, and signature synergy. Keep catalogue size. | All kits validate, contain four distinct spells, cover native ingredients, and offer a coherent reason to select that character. |
| Characters and races | Canonical identity, assets and unique signatures are preserved. | Inherited `roster-v7` descriptions promise stamina, racial perks, different health and recovery, while the engine gives all champions HP8, Flux6 and movement4. | Replace current descriptions through a versioned wrapper with truthful tactical roles. Race remains identity, not an invented hidden mechanic. | No current description promises nonexistent stats/perks; affinity and signature identity remain unchanged. |
| Team composition | Three-character teams and pre-match kits keep battle choice bounded. | Generic kits expose many attacks but too few deliberate support roles; team coverage is useful only if its ingredients are actually equipped. | Curate complementary kits; preserve actual equipped-element coverage and free camp customization. | A fresh company can protect, displace, damage and combine fields without changing 12 slots first. |
| Affinity progression | Unique Story victories grant roster-wide points; free respec avoids a permanent build mistake. | `budgetFor` reaches 18 after 13 wins, while `encounterAffinities` tops out at 14. UI does not guide challenge selection; rank0 training previews rank2 spells though next click buys rank1. | Preserve the user's earned-affinity policy and fixed foe strength; make next point's real effect explicit and recommend unfinished encounters by current company strength. Defer curve changes until observed play. | No loss of earned progress; no hidden enemy scaling; recommendation explains strength and can be ignored; next-point preview is numerically correct. |
| Resources and statuses | Bounded Flux, recovery, Resolve and environmental damage avoid unrestricted loops. | Long descriptions list many statuses without showing which part matters now; story reward preview evaluates every creator at rank5. | Correct reward preview to actual encounter rank and player unlock context; leave numerical resource model to systems tests. | Preview damage/range/duration equals the actual opponent spell; no claimed stronger effect from the wrong allocation. |
| Objectives | Wells and defeats yield a stable, easily compared goal. | Thirty encounters use the same formal objective, yet introductions are mostly unrelated creature anecdotes. | Keep the scoring objective; pair each introduction with a specific practical problem using existing geometry, signature or creature. | Each lesson names an action or tradeoff, without suggesting an extra hidden victory condition. |
| Maps and terrain | Connected mirrored seeds, biomes and high ground provide repeatable geography. | Very large field and distant starts can hide relevant wells and creatures from a novice's first camera view. | Preserve requested map scale; focus initial camera and onboarding on useful routes/nearby objectives instead of enlarging further. | First-turn useful destination is discoverable; no map shrink/expansion is needed to explain the objective. |
| Procedural generation | Fixed seeds and deterministic inhabitant perches are reproducible. | `storyInhabitants` picks safe perches near wells but does not guarantee each advertised creature affects a contested decision. | Retain reproducibility and safety; diagnostic cases should inspect distance to actual routes and observed pulse relevance. Defer new generator families. | No invalid perch, spawn exposure or disconnected pocket; record inactive creatures rather than equating presence with relevance. |
| Balance and integrity | Equal affinity is explicit and validated in competitive rooms. | Equal totals are not equal utility; story power cap overtakes all authored opponents; limited diagnostics used only two teams. | Preserve totals as the user's gauge, state its limits, broaden fixed-seed composition diagnostics. | Seat-swapped comparisons, legal plans and no unexplained affinity advantage; no claim of solved competitive balance. |
| Story | Thirty encounters include existing champions, 63 named creatures and signature rewards. | Selector groups by element while difficulty/creature complexity follow original roster index; there is no intelligible recommended journey. | Add a non-locking recommended next challenge with an exact practical lesson; retain full encounter access and all earned rewards. | New profile has a clear first encounter; completed encounters are not recommended; higher-strength choices remain voluntary. |
| AI | Public-field planning and explicit creature counters already exist. | `encounterLoadouts` replaces slot2 with the highest available advanced spell, potentially deleting a useful support ingredient and flattening roles. | Use authored role-aware upgrade candidates and preserve elemental coverage; evaluate AI using those actual kits. | Every encounter opponent kit is legal, retains its intended role, and has purposeful differences from other champions. |
| Multiplayer | Sealed orders, retries and legacy dispatch protect trust. | Changing spell affinity access without a new rule version would reinterpret existing choices and pending games. | Use rules13 and preserve frozen12; do not send creatures as controllable units or silently change old kits. | Old room/save resolves unchanged; new join/plan validation rejects off-affinity spells consistently. |
| Onboarding | Default playable company, visible creatures and recipe exploration exist. | First screen exposes three champions and fifteen actions; useful tactical explanation lives in closed `Opponent tactics`. | Give one relevant lesson and suggested challenge, not a mandatory tutorial carousel or more modes. | A novice can find a well, queue a move plus action, understand simultaneity and predict one field change without reading all rules. |
| Interface | Current version has one commit surface and collapsed detail. | Story signature preview uses the wrong rank; training hides next effect in a tooltip; duplicated title/summary text consumes attention. | Put decision-changing information adjacent to its control, remove redundant prose. | The next click's consequence is visible on touch; summary and detail do not contradict actual data. |
| Controls and mobile | Keyboard, Undo, camera focus and SVG target labels exist. | Hover-only detail cannot teach on phones, and the large board often requires camera work before action. | Keep native controls and direct touch-readable lessons; experience owner should validate real action journeys. | Move, cast, undo, inspect and commit at 320px without hover dependence or accidental orders during pan. |
| Visuals and animation | Original environment atlases and 13 creature body families give a coherent base. | Desires describe living habits but inhabitants remain rooted periodic emitters. This is acceptable only if presentation does not promise autonomous ecology. | Retain bodies and grounded anticipation; defer wandering/quests until they serve a tactical need. | Anticipation identifies actual pending action; final claims distinguish 63 identities from 63 bespoke animation sheets. |
| Audio | Material voices and bounded priority avoid uncontrolled sound overlap. | Oscillator profiles are tested, but no listening evidence establishes warmth, separation or fatigue. | Retain opt-in audio; evaluate listening separately rather than add music as a proxy for quality. | Muted game remains clear; actual listening checks report intelligibility and fatigue instead of counting cues. |
| Accessibility | Text labels, status names, focus and reduced motion exist. | Long compound/status descriptions and icon-only affordances may create cognitive or nonvisual load. | Plain exact role/lesson language, text plus shapes, no mandatory color decoding. | Lessons understandable without color; controls announce action and current value; human accessibility claims remain qualified. |
| Pacing and replayability | Fixed encounters can be replayed with improved builds and learned signatures. | Power gains outpace fixed difficulty, and recommender absence makes “what next?” unclear; a larger catalogue alone does not create replay interest. | Recommend appropriate unfinished challenges, show next point consequences, curate roles; defer more content/modes. | Returning player finds a meaningful next challenge and can change strategy without chores or repeat reward farming. |
| Validation | Extensive deterministic tests and published HTTP checks support correctness. | Previous evidence verifies coverage and two browser exchanges, not independent novice comprehension or sustained enjoyment. | Add content legality/identity/recommendation/preview tests, full-match diagnostics and actual browser journey; clearly reserve fun claims for humans. | Evidence differentiates automated checks, browser operation, physical device, listening and player observation. |

## Implementation proposals accepted for integration

1. Current-version native-affinity spell access, with legal migration and historical preservation.
2. Thirty curated starting kits and truthful role descriptions; no extra spells, currencies or hidden race bonuses.
3. Authored encounter kits that evolve those roles without blindly replacing a slot.
4. Recommended unfinished Story challenge and one useful lesson per encounter; no locks or compulsory tutorial screens.
5. Accurate boss signature and next-affinity-point previews, preserving existing earned points and fixed opponent strength.

These changes make the existing systems easier to learn and give decisions more character. The difficult remaining question is whether players find the prediction, movement and shared landscape satisfying over repeated matches. That requires observed play; no amount of record uniqueness proves it.

## Implemented world changes and independent integration review

The pure `character-design-v13.ts` table now provides 30 different four-spell starter kits, tactical roles, accurate descriptions and explicit same-element upgrade candidates. Each kit covers every canonical native ingredient and offers a usable attack plus a non-damage tactical consequence. The descriptions do not invent race modifiers, stamina or unequal baseline statistics. The display-only `roster-v13.ts` wrapper preserves all original identity, affinity and asset data.

Story remains 30 freely selectable encounters with fixed opponent affinity, the same 63 inhabitants, the same reward rules and the same browser profile key. Every encounter now has one practical lesson. Recommendations compare unfinished encounters to the selected company's actually allocated affinity; unspent budget does not inflate strength. Story signatures are evaluated at the actual opponent's allocation, with explicit wording that a learned spell scales from the player's own allocation. Next-point training previews now show rank one correctly, with touch-readable unlock and scaling effects.

`tests/world-design-v13.test.ts`: **7 passed, 0 failed**. These are broad behavioral tests, not one assertion per record: all 30 current identities and native starter kits; all 30 created/restored encounters and role upgrades; all 30 actual-boss previews; recommendation behavior across completion and allocation; all 36 next-point unlock thresholds; saved profile/custom-slot preservation; strict versioned reward and replay behavior. Typecheck and integrated release evidence are recorded separately by the integration owner.

Cross-review with the systems owner confirmed the new force contract: sum/cancel force, subtract one for Guard, then cap movement at two steps; existing Anchored prevents displacement. Story advice about stronger pushes was rewritten accordingly. A focused test caught Treevor's first proposed kit omitting Fire; Cauterize restored his native ingredient while retaining useful care. The actual-rank preview test also caught an incorrect assumed base range in the test fixture; its assertion now derives the authoritative base signature rather than inventing a number.
