# Rules 13: fourth independent challenge review

Reviewed 2026-10-18 against published baseline `eb572976`, the actual rules 12
engine, spell access, roster, Story, public planning helpers, feedback, audio and
room dispatch. Rules 13 files were scaffolding at this review. This is the fourth
independent review of **all 24 areas**, not a review of only transport. No browser,
production build or new playtest was performed by this reviewer at this stage.

## Independent diagnosis

The strongest identity is a small company changing shared terrain through
simultaneous choices. The current obstacles are not a lack of systems: they are
universal low-level spell access diluting company identity, Guard completely
negating positional tools, indistinct algorithmic starter kits, and explanation
that often arrives after a player needed it. More characters, phases, currencies,
terrain rules or effects would add cost before addressing these problems.

The bounded proposed overhaul is coherent if existing matches retain their old
rules, warnings distinguish certainty from possibility, and default kits remain
flexible starting points rather than compulsory classes. A recommendation or
intent count must save attention without introducing another confirmation step.

## Three strongest counterarguments to resolve

1. **Restricting level-one access may improve identity but silently invalidate old
   games.** In the baseline, `rooms.normalizeLoadouts` sends every rules version
   >=11 through one spell validator. Merely replacing that import changes the
   meaning of a saved rules 12 squad before its frozen resolver runs. Dispatch the
   validator by rules version as well as the engine. Preserve old zero-affinity
   kits in old games; repair them only on a deliberately requested rules 13 start.
   Test both unlocked custom slots and the raw pending-plan/retry fingerprint.

2. **One-step resistance is ambiguous unless its algebra is defined.** The engine
   sums possibly fractional axial vectors and caps execution at two steps. Guard
   should reduce net displacement after opposing forces cancel, then apply the
   existing cap, without rounding each contributing cast separately. A remaining
   step can trigger an Ice slide to a farther cell, so "Guard moves you at most
   one hex" would be false. Keep "resists one displacement step" and explain Ice
   separately. Existing Anchored cancels all displacement; newly applied Anchored
   must not retroactively protect the same exchange.

3. **A more helpful interface can become a new explanation burden or false
   oracle.** Public-field forecasting cannot guarantee a final result: unseen
   enemy movement/casts can alter it. Allied collisions are certain under the
   visible plan; rival occupancy, predicted field damage and counterspell success
   are conditional. Keep those severity levels explicit, rank only the most
   consequential warnings, and leave Guard implicit without mandatory completion
   gates. Prioritize actual defeat/capture/fizzle outcomes before creature flavor.

## Scope boundary

Recommended: proceed with this focused rules 13 overhaul and the evidence above.
Not recommended in this pass: more map size, new elemental subsets, race passives,
new resources, new turn phases, mandatory tutorial sequences, hidden adaptive
Story difficulty, or claims of competitive balance and human enjoyment derived
only from automated matches.

Implementation cross-review and transport evidence will be appended after the
candidate is integrated. This document's current conclusions are independent
source-based design findings, not a claim that the proposed changes already pass.
