Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a single original game-magic design/reference sheet, not a finished runtime spritesheet.

Primary request: Design one cohesive family of eight elemental spell effects for FLUX, an original top-down pixel action game. Combine the charming, economical silhouettes and tactile small-color clusters of classic handheld top-down adventures such as The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages/Seasons with the punchy, instantly readable impact design associated with Enter the Gungeon. Borrow only general visual principles; create original shapes. Do not copy any game's sprite, character, UI, logo, weapon or emblem.

Composition: one tall portrait reference board, ideally 1536 by 2048. EXACTLY eight orderly rows and EXACTLY three artwork columns, making 24 clearly separated studies. Use a narrow left label gutter. All cells have equal space and generous padding. No overlapping effects between cells. Title at top: "EIGHT ELEMENTS". Column headers, spelled exactly once: "PROJECTILE", "CONTACT BURST", "GROUND DEPOSIT". Row labels down the left in this exact order: "FIRE", "WATER", "EARTH", "WIND", "CHARGE", "ICE", "LIGHT", "DARK". Legible restrained pixel lettering, no other text.

Scene/backdrop: flat dark slate-charcoal board, without scenery, decorative frame, gradients, paper texture or checkerboard. A faint regular dividing grid is acceptable. Show artwork sharply at a consistent enlarged pixel scale.

Style/medium: charming hand-clustered pixel-art concept language; chunky clean stair-stepped edges, controlled 3-to-5-color ramps, restrained dark outlines, strong solid silhouettes and a few carefully placed highlight pixels. Playful and tactile without faces. No soft painting, airbrushed glow, bloom, lens flare, noisy particle confetti, realistic rendering or vector-like smoothing. Keep light effects bright by shape and contrast, not a large blurred halo. Shapes must distinguish elements even without color.

The three roles must look different:
- PROJECTILE: compact directional traveling body pointing right, with a short leftward tail that communicates motion; keep the core dominant.
- CONTACT BURST: one instantaneous compact impact pose expanding around a center, with 3-to-7 bold material lobes/fragments/rays and very limited detached particles. It must be visibly different from a projectile.
- GROUND DEPOSIT: a small square top-down material texture study intended to guide a repeatable ground tile. Use a low, horizontal distribution rather than a tall explosion; suggest edge-compatible repeatable motifs, without a fixed circular gameplay boundary, raised pedestal or isometric block. This is a texture concept, not a claim of verified seamless tiling.

Eight element identities and project palette targets (dark / base / bright):
1. FIRE: orange-red forked flame tongues with warm yellow tips; energetic tapered curls and a compact ember material tile. #5b1d12 / #dd5930 / #ffd169.
2. WATER: saturated blue rounded lobed splashes, a plump curling droplet projectile, scalloped contact splash, and low connected ripples/puddled texture. #0c3d57 / #368cf0 / #7de2ef.
3. EARTH: ochre angular stone chunks, a weighty fractured rock projectile, chunky broken contact shards, and compact cracked stone/grit ground motifs. #44321f / #936f3f / #d4b06b.
4. WIND: pale mint open curved gust ribbons with clear empty spaces, airy arcing contact sweeps, and sparse repeating shallow wind curls. Never water droplets or crystals. #25524d / #67cf92 / #c8f2d4.
5. CHARGE: warm golden angular broken lightning sparks; sharp stepped zigzags and snapped branching strokes, distinctly warmer than Light. Ground motif is a sparse branching electric trace, not a sun. #70550f / #e2b82f / #fff19a.
6. ICE: crisp cyan crystalline teeth, a compact faceted ice shard, contact chips and six-point accents, and flat angular frost facets/teeth on the ground. Distinctly harder and paler than Water. #214d74 / #91e6ef / #d5f4ff.
7. LIGHT: warm ivory clean starbursts and radiant diamonds, a compact four-point traveling star, a measured radial contact flash, and sparse softly ordered luminous star/diamond ground motifs. Ivory dominant, not golden lightning. #777353 / #fff1c7 / #fff7c2.
8. DARK: violet inky smoke with concave inward curls and dense dark cores, an inward-crescent projectile, an expanding but hollow curling ink burst, and low pooling ink/smoke swirls. Preserve readable violet edges on the slate background. #160d24 / #9450c9 / #ad6dd1.

Constraints: keep all 24 studies readable, cohesive and unclipped. No figures, hands, weapons, UI meters, damage numbers, arrows added outside the artwork, logos, watermarks, game screenshots or additional rows. This is one style-direction board with single poses, not an animation sequence.
